Verbalex Support

Help with the Verbalex app for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email us at momsy.app.support@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem.

Subscriptions

Verbalex Premium is billed by Apple. To manage or cancel it, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

To restore a purchase, open Profile > Verbalex Premium in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

On-device AI

AI features need a device with Apple Intelligence enabled. Everything else works on any supported device.

Your data

Verbalex has no accounts. Your words and progress stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy.
