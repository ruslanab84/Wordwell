Privacy Policy

Verbalex for iPhone and iPad. Last updated November 9, 2026.

Summary

Verbalex has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Data on your device

Your level, settings, saved words, search history and practice progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Device backups you make may include them.

Microphone and speech

If you use speaking practice, iOS asks for access to the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is transcribed on your device and is not sent to us. You can turn off this access at any time in iOS Settings.

On-device AI

AI features use Apple Intelligence models that run on your device. Your text is not sent to us or to a third-party AI service.

Notifications

Word notifications are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple processes purchases and manages subscriptions under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Contact

For privacy questions, email momsy.app.support@gmail.com or visit Verbalex Support.
