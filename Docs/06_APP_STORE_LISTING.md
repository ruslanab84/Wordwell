## App Review Notes (limit 4000)

```
No account or sign-in is required. After a short onboarding, all features are available from the tab bar.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Verbalex Premium has two auto-renewable subscriptions in one group: wordwell.premium.monthly and wordwell.premium.yearly. "wordwell" is the internal project identifier.
To open the paywall: Profile > Verbalex Premium. It also opens from Settings, from locked content, and when a free daily limit is reached. Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are on the paywall.

FREE TIER
Free users get the full dictionary, 16 A1 grammar lessons, 3 practice sessions per day and 3 AI requests per day. Premium removes these limits and unlocks all grammar lessons, English Skills Check results, Word for You and the Home topic filter.

ON-DEVICE AI
AI features use Apple's Foundation Models framework and run entirely on the device. They need a device with Apple Intelligence enabled. On devices that do not support Apple Intelligence, AI features are hidden and the rest of the app works normally. On supported devices where Apple Intelligence is turned off, the app asks the user to turn it on in the Settings app. The app does not generate learning content when the model is unavailable.
To try AI: Practice > Conversation, Practice > Improve my sentence, or Practice > Quiz Me.

MICROPHONE AND SPEECH RECOGNITION
Used only for speaking practice (Practice > Speaking and English Skills Check). Speech is transcribed on the device.

DATA AND NETWORK
The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not send user data anywhere. User data stays on the device. Purchases are handled by Apple.

CONTENT
The dictionary is bundled with the app and based on Open English WordNet (CC BY 4.0). Credit and a source link appear on each dictionary entry. As a complete general-purpose dictionary it includes vulgar words as entries.
English Skills Check is an independent practice tool and is not affiliated with any exam provider.

WIDGET
The Word of the Day widget is available in Small, Medium and Lock Screen sizes.
```
